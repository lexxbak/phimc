PhiMC

01 / Overview

What the contest was

PhiMC ran entirely online, aimed at giving younger students low-stakes exposure to contest math. Every problem required a written numerical answer — no multiple choice — and the whole event, including proctoring, lived inside a Google Classroom.

AudienceGrades 4–8
DifficultyAMC 8 → AMC 10
ProctoringQuilgo
Answer typeWritten, no MC
Google Classroom — closed to new sign-ups Official Discord phimathcontest@gmail.com
02 / Format

Three rounds, in order

Students moved through the weekend in a fixed sequence: everyone sat the individual round, teams of up to three then sat a shorter team round, and only students who needed to be separated at the top of the standings were sent a tiebreaker afterward.

01

Individual Round

Open to every registered student automatically upon joining the Classroom. The core of the contest.

25 Q45 minutes
02

Team Round

Teams of up to three, signed up separately through a form posted in the Classroom.

10 Q20 minutes
03

Tiebreaker

Sent privately only to students who needed separating at the top of the standings.

5 Q20 minutes
03 / Eligibility

Who could take part

The contest was open wide, but prize eligibility was reserved for its core audience.

Grades 4–8

The intended audience, and the only students eligible to win prizes.

High schoolers

Welcome to compete and get the experience, but not eligible for prizes.

International students

Free to participate from anywhere, also outside the prize pool.

04 / Special events

Around the contest

Two live gatherings bookended the testing window — a guest lecture mid-contest, and a results premiere with giveaways once scoring closed.

Mar 6, 2021 · 1:00 PM PT

BeautyofMath Lecture

A live Zoom talk held partway through the testing window, open to all registered participants.

Mar 7, 2021 · 5:00 PM CT

Results Premiere with ThePuzzlr

Results were revealed live on ThePuzzlr's YouTube channel, alongside a walkthrough of standout problems and a run of giveaways. Winners needed to be present at the premiere to claim their prize.
